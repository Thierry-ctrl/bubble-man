**My judgement — fixed design choices below:**

1. **Zones:** among previously assessable DMAs, rank by exact count of usable 30-minute bins after the unchanged reader; ties by numeric DMA ID ascending. Select the first five. Do not choose by warning counts, flow variability, repairs or injection performance. No replacement based on results.
2. **Starts:** 200 distinct 30-minute-grid start times per zone, uniformly sampled without replacement. Seed `20261018`, with a separate NumPy generator seeded by `[20261018, numeric_DMA_ID]`. Sample from day index 56 through the final timestamp minus 168 hours, inclusive. Reuse these dates for every size and both rules. Include dates with later missing readings or existing warnings; no favourable-window filtering.
3. **Injected shape:** one abrupt, constant extra rate from its start through the file end, on each separate copy of the original series. Add to finite bins only; preserve every gap. Never stack injections. Full past state, learned profiles, arming, spacing and adaptive behaviour run unchanged. The first 28 learning/threshold days are untouched.
4. **Sizes:** absolute rates `0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 m³/h`; also `5, 10, 20, 40, 80%` of a readings-only minimum-night-flow reference. Merge duplicate sizes. No grid expansion after outcomes. If the reference is nonpositive/unavailable, report relative sizes unavailable and retain that zone's absolute results.
5. **Reference:** median of the daily minimum complete two-hour mean between 00:00 and 06:00, using days 29–365 with all six hourly means present. This descriptive minimum-night-flow proxy includes both customer use and unknown existing leakage. It is not a loss estimate or an input to the detector; percentages scale the artificial additions only.
6. **Responses:** primary = at least one warning within 168 hours whose issue time is absent from the paired untouched run. This can be an extra or retimed warning. Its final contributing bin must be at/after onset (`issue − 30 min ≥ onset`). Report secondary any-warning probability too, alongside its untouched-series coincidence probability. Do not reset the rule at onset. Unchanged-timing warnings do not count as primary responses, even if their step becomes larger.
7. **Probability/delay:** empirical successes out of 200 random dates for each size/rule/zone; median and 25th–75th percentile delay among primary responders. Nonresponders remain in the probability denominator and are not assigned a fake 168-hour delay. Report the fraction of starts with complete seven-night observation opportunities. No inferential field-accuracy claim or sampling confidence guarantee.
8. **Minimum at seven days:** smallest **tested** size for which primary response is at least 90% and remains at least 90% at every larger tested size. Report m³/h and percent of the reference separately for each rule. If none, report “90% not reached on the tested grid.” This is a descriptive convention, not a utility acceptance target. No interpolation, fitted smoothing or extrapolation.
9. **Untouched workload:** count warnings from day index 28 to year end; give calendar exposure, available step nights and annualised warnings (`count × 365 / 337`). Also report the random-date seven-day coincidence rate. These are warning rates, not false-alarm rates; the real background may contain leaks.
